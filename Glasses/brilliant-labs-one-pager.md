# Brilliant Labs — one pager

**From:** George Gally (radarboy3000)
**What:** A partnership for dev units, firmware access and a joint demo.
**Ask:** Five Frame dev kits, early access to firmware and SDK changes, a monthly call with someone on your side, and permission to film and show the result.

## What I'm building

Fashion-first glasses with a real intelligence inside them. Retro-futuristic frames — 1970s and 80s, Rams, Space Odyssey, Teenage Engineering, thick plastic, dark orange lenses — because the frame is the product, not the display.

The first behaviour is simple enough to film in 20 seconds: you glance at something you like and it is in your phone, identified, priced and buyable. No assistant talking, no head-up display clutter. One mark in the lens and a taste graph that gets sharper the longer you wear them.

## Why this is worth your dev units

- I designed and built Cartier's Looking Glass — GAN-based AR jewellery try-on, patent pending, covered by Wired, Fast Company and Vogue Business. The hard part of AR is not the render, it's the interaction, and that part I have already shipped inside a luxury house.
- Before that: Senior Creative Technologist, Cartier Brooklyn Innovation Lab; Ogilvy Japan; TBWA. Twenty-five years of building interfaces people actually want to look at.
- I am not asking you for hardware you'd have to invent. Frame already has the camera, the IMU and an open SDK. What I unlock is a product story your platform doesn't yet have: taste, not utility.
- Whatever I learn on the dev kits — heat, battery cost of camera-over-BLE, what breaks on a face in daylight — I'll share back, publicly where it's useful to your community.
