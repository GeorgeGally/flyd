## Top 3 highest-leverage signals

1. **Git activity (#1) joined to Claude Code session titles (#2).** Both are live, already in code and local. Together they say what George built, where he got stuck and what he abandoned. They cost little: fix re-observation of the stale repos and ignore the 271 automated sessions.
2. **The ArtAndTech / Invention Studio repo (#4), then his ChatGPT/Claude chats (#3).** This is where his current creative thinking actually lives, it is fresh to the hour, and it is not wired. Connecting it as a consented personal source (read-only, via `gh`) is the single biggest jump from "reports my work" to "knows what I am thinking". The chat importer already exists for ChatGPT; it needs one export file, plus a Claude.ai equivalent.
3. **Liesel boards (#5), read as taste votes.** Live, visual, and what his taste rules are made of. Today Flyd only opens them.

## What Flyd could have surprised George with this week

From real evidence: George spent the week in cap5 (188 commits), flyd (188), imogen (106) and hftw (97), and on 23 Nov rebuilt pages boards for cap5 and cleanx. On 24 Nov he and Manolis wrote the Invention Studio founding charter and three commercial models, and the repo itself says the homepage copy is a first draft and the portfolio list needs verification. A proactive Flyd could have done this unasked: matched the portfolio inventory against what he actually shipped (cap5, imogen, dead-internet-radio, cleanx, plus flyd itself), pulled a shot of each from the Liesel boards, and put a studio homepage canvas in front of him on the morning of the 24th, with copy rewritten from the charter in his own taste (short, plain, visual), and three outcome choices for the unresolved questions. It could also have said that the artandtech folder in Documents and the GitHub repo of nearly the same name are different things, and offered to tidy that. Separately: imogen and hftw have had no commit for two to three days after a heavy run, so a "want me to pick one up?" nudge fits.

## Caveats

- No chat export was found locally; I did not look inside ChatGPT or Claude.ai accounts.
- Freshness comes from file mtimes and counts, not content audits; I did not read transcript bodies, board contents beyond item counts, or repo files beyond README and heading lines.
