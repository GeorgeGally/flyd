# What Flyd does without asking
label: wayfinder:grilling
status: resolved
blocked-by: 01-proactive-signals

## Question

Where is the line for proactive work: what Flyd and its teams may start, finish and ship unasked (reversible, in his taste), what waits for a creative review, what always waits for George (money, public or client-facing, irreversible), and how Flyd 'pushes and inspires' him rather than just doing chores.

## Answer (George, 2026-11-24)

- Flyd and its teams start any reversible work in his taste unasked (fixes, drafts, experiments, research, tidying), on a side copy, never on live work.
- They finish and ship unasked in his own projects: land, push to GitHub, merge green work.
- Creative reviews: anything with a visible look or voice. Every big piece of work becomes a report presented to George, but it does not need him: work keeps moving on the recommended option, and he can overrule later.
- Always waits for George: money, anything public or client-facing, anything irreversible, deploys to held live sites, security or credentials.
- Pushing and inspiring: Flyd brings something worth reacting to (an idea, an experiment already running, a reference from his own work). It can do this at any time, not only at day or project start. Only when genuinely new.
- Every decision made for him gets one plain line: what was decided and why. Reversible.
