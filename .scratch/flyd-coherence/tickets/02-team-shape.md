# What a firstmate-shaped team is
label: wayfinder:research
status: closed (2026-11-24)
blocked-by: -

## Question

What exactly makes firstmate a team (lead, workers, backlog, supervision, reporting, secondmates, briefs, landing), and what would it take to stand up Design studio, Research & news desk, Writing and Operations in the same shape? Which parts of firstmate are reusable as-is (secondmate homes, dispatch, backlog), which are engineering-specific, and how would a CoS give each team work and read its results without relayed chat?

## Resolution

Research: [02-team-shape](../research/02-team-shape.md). Firstmate's generic parts (secondmate homes, brief, backlog, status + report files) carry over; landing/worktrees stay engineering-only. Relays fail mainly on a strict brief/report/evidence regex applied to prose replies (acceptance.ts:199-289, firstmate.ts:762-796) and on Flyd launching a firstmate session per note. Contract: CoS writes a brief; team writes status + result.json; Flyd reads records, never chat. Stand up Research desk first, then Writing, Design, Operations last; Engineering stays firstmate unchanged.
