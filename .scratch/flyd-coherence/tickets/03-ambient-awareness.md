# How George stays ambiently aware
label: wayfinder:grilling
status: closed (2026-11-24)
blocked-by: -

## Question

How does George always know what's going on without being bombarded, so he trusts Flyd instead of going to firstmate? What is always visible, what arrives as a gentle update, what earns an interruption, how fast Flyd must tell him things, and how Flyd shows it is on top of things (trust signals).

## Resolution

Decided with George 2026-11-24:
- Interruptions: only a client waiting, money at risk, something broken that's live, or a creative review holding work up. Everything else is ambient.
- One live line per job, changing as things change (like the Claude/ChatGPT mobile app's status line), appearing within seconds of a request.
- Where it lives: the sidebar was meant for this. Open to better: a dynamic-island-style rollover (Hey Clicky does this but badly) or a restrained ambient 3D surface, "like Jeeves" with less sci-fi design. Earlier 3D attempts did not work, but the direction is still right.
- Reaching it: ambient or on demand, never asking him to OK or close anything. A project shortcut in chat (e.g. /flyd) brings up an overview (3D carousel or board).
- Proof: shown intentionally like a presentation to an ECD or client, not whole-page screenshots he has to decode.
- Detail: tapping a line opens its canvas or thread. Possibly a news-ticker-style strip (George likes the idea, suspects it is too much).
- Two daily briefs (morning, end of day): accepted.
- Flyd's current dynamic-island notifications are bad and mostly meaningless; to be redesigned.
