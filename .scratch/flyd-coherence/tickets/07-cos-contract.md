# Flyd, Chief of Staff and teams: who does what
label: wayfinder:grilling
status: resolved
blocked-by: 02-team-shape

## Question

The operating contract: what Flyd owns (George, taste, creative direction, supervising the CoS, walking the studio, sitting in meetings), what the CoS owns (planning, team leads, managing firstmate, reporting up), how work and results move between them as shared records instead of relayed messages, and what Flyd sees when it walks the studio.

## Answer (George, 2026-11-24)

- Flyd owns: George, his taste and creative direction, supervising the Chief of Staff, walking the studio, sitting in meetings. It does not plan or manage work itself.
- The Chief of Staff owns: planning, team leads, managing firstmate as one specialist team among others, reporting up to Flyd.
- Work and results move through shared records every layer reads (one record per piece of work: goal, owner, state, result), never relayed messages.
- Flyd walks the studio by reading those records directly, and reports to George only what is new, in plain words.
