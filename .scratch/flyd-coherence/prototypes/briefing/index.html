What you need to know

Three rough takes on one briefing: what's done, what's being tried, what was decided and why, and the looks Flyd wants your eye on. None of it needs you. Work keeps moving on Flyd's pick and you can overrule any time.

A. In the chatFlyd posts the briefing as a message, with the looks just below it. Picking another option or reversing a decision gets an answer in chat. B. A report per piece of workEach big piece of work arrives in chat as one card. It opens into a presentation-style report that holds the same four things for that work. C. Morning and evening digestTwo editions a day across every project, with the finished work as tiles and the looks beside what's running.

Recommendation

Use A day to day, and B when a big piece of work finishes. The briefing in chat stays short and covers everything across projects. A finished body of work arrives as a B card in that same chat and opens into its report. C's two editions are just A at 07:30 and 18:30, so they don't need a page of their own.
