# Plan the days

1. Start from what is fixed: his calendar, due reminders, and the deadlines from his projects. Look them up if they aren't in front of you.
2. Put the hardest work where his energy is highest and before its deadline, with a buffer day before anything that can't slip.
3. Honour every constraint he gives, literally. "Spaced with rest days" means no two on consecutive days. Check the plan against each constraint before you answer, and fix it if one breaks.
4. Keep it light: day, time, the thing. One line per day, and skip empty days unless he asked for every day.
5. Don't invent commitments he never mentioned.
6. Offer one step: put it in his calendar, or set reminders for the fixed points.
