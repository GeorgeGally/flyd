# Get it launched

A launch is several different jobs wearing one word. Name them, split them, and make the first step small.

1. From his project notes (not the code, unless he asks), list what "launched" actually requires. The usual list: merge and deploy, store or marketplace submission and review time, pricing and payments, the landing page, the announcement, and the first users.
2. Split the list into what only George can do (decisions, accounts, money, his voice) and what Flyd can do (code on the crew, drafts, checklists, research, scheduling). Most of the list should be Flyd's.
3. Put the critical path first. Anything with a lead time (store review often takes days) starts today, whatever else is unfinished.
4. Offer to start Flyd's part now: code changes go to the crew, drafts and research to a background job. Anything that pushes, deploys, publishes, or spends money gets his yes first.
5. When several launches land in the same week, name the one to finish first and say why: the nearest deadline, lead times, or what earns money.
