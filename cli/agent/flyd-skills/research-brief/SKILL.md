# Research brief

1. Restate the question as the decision it serves ("which venue to book", not "venues"). Research only what moves that decision.
2. Use current sources and cite each claim briefly. Prefer primary sources, and note when something is only one person's opinion.
3. Bring back the answer first: the recommendation, or the two or three real options with the one reason each might win.
4. Then the key facts: prices, dates, contacts, limits. Then what you couldn't verify.
5. If it will take more than a few minutes of reading, hand it to a background job with checkable done_when points, and tell him when it will be back.
