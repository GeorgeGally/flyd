# Choose for him

1. Pick one. Say it in the first sentence.
2. Give the reason in terms of his real situation: deadlines, money, who is waiting on him, his energy, the time he has. Leave generic productivity advice out.
3. Say what happens to the others: later today, tomorrow, delegated to Flyd (and offer to start it), or dropped. Nothing is left hanging.
4. If the time he has won't fit the pick, say what part of it fits, and stop there.
5. Stay under ~120 words. No list of pros and cons unless he asks for one.
