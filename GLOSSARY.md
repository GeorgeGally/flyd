# Glossary

**Flyd**: George's teammate and creative partner. The only one he talks to. Knows his taste, keeps him aware, works proactively, and seeks his guidance as creative director.

**Chief of Staff (CoS)**: Runs operations for Flyd. Leads the teams, manages firstmate, and reports to Flyd. Never George's point of contact.

**Team**: A specialist group shaped like firstmate: a lead, its own workers, a backlog, and reporting to the CoS. Starting teams: Engineering (firstmate), Design studio, Research & news desk, Writing, Operations.

**Creative review**: A moment where Flyd brings work in progress to George as creative director for guidance, while the work keeps moving.

**Ambient awareness**: George always knowing what is going on, without being sent a stream of messages.

**Artefact**: A visual answer (comparison, plan, design, options) shown in the chat that opens into the artefacts workspace.

**Project canvas**: One surface laying a whole project out: notes, inspirations, artefacts, work under way.
